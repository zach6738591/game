# Safety & Ethics Guardrails

This project must never be used to provide real-world tactical or operational instruction. All collaborators agree to the following guardrails:

1. **Civilian Protection First**: Mechanics, documentation, and assets must prioritize civilian well-being, humanitarian aid, and de-escalation.
2. **No Weaponization**: Do not include realistic weapon schematics, procurement references, or violent playbooks. Non-lethal tools remain abstract and symbolic.
3. **Fictionalization & Privacy**: Use fictional names, locations, and timelines when expanding content. Avoid referencing identifiable individuals.
4. **Transparency & Accountability**: Every release includes content warnings, citations to educational resources, and a call for independent ethics review.
5. **Educational Framing**: Position the experience as a learning tool about conflict prevention, diplomacy, and civic responsibility.
6. **Accessibility & Inclusion**: Ensure options for varied abilities, languages, and cultural perspectives.
7. **Review Process**: Submit major updates to a multidisciplinary panel (ethics, legal, community advisors) before distribution.

By contributing, you acknowledge responsibility for upholding these safeguards.
